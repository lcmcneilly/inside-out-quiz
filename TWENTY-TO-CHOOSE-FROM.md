# TWENTY TO CHOOSE FROM

Written to the register of the four you kept: a concrete scene, then something
involuntary; proportion rather than identity; short plain options; C is never
a wrong answer.

Four per territory, so you can pick a balanced set. Territory names are
internal — they never appear on screen.

---

## THE VERDICT
*How fast the judgement arrives, and that the quickest one is usually about you.*

**V1.** You read something someone has written about their own life. Which happens first?
- A. I notice what I would have done differently
- B. Depends what they wrote
- C. I just take it in

**V2.** Someone does something you would never do. Which is closer?
- A. I know what that says about them
- B. It depends on the person
- C. I assume there is a reason I cannot see

**V3.** You make a mistake nobody else notices. What happens to it?
- A. I carry it around for a while
- B. It fades by the next day
- C. It is gone almost immediately

**V4.** Someone praises work you know was not your best. What happens?
- A. I correct them, or want to
- B. I take it and move on
- C. I let it be theirs to think

---

## THE MEASURING
*Comparison, and where you are standing when you do it.*

**M1.** You open your phone and the first thing you see is somebody else's good news. What do you do?
- A. Keep going past it
- B. Look, then move on
- C. Stop and read it properly

**M2.** Think of someone a few steps ahead of you. Which is closer?
- A. I know exactly how far ahead
- B. Roughly
- C. I have never worked it out

**M3.** Somebody asks how your year has been. What do you measure it against before you answer?
- A. Where I thought I would be
- B. Last year
- C. Nothing. I just answer

**M4.** A friend tells you something good that has happened to them. When does the gladness arrive?
- A. A beat after the other thing
- B. Straight away, mostly
- C. Immediately, every time

---

## WHERE STEADINESS IS KEPT
*How much of it somebody else is currently holding.*

**S1.** You send something you worked hard on. What happens in the gap before they reply?
- A. I reread it, or check for an answer
- B. A bit of both
- C. I am already onto the next thing

**S2.** Someone close to you is in a bad mood that has nothing to do with you. How long before you are sure it is not about you?
- A. Longer than I would like
- B. A few minutes
- C. I do not go there

**S3.** Think of the last decision you were pleased with. Who knew before you felt settled about it?
- A. Someone. I needed to say it out loud
- B. One or two people, eventually
- C. Nobody. It was settled before I told anyone

**S4.** A message is left on read. Which is closer?
- A. I build a reason
- B. I notice, then let it go
- C. I do not really register it

## THE REASONS GIVEN
*The ones you would defend out loud.*

**R1.** Something you want is one decision away. What is the decision waiting on?
- A. The right moment
- B. One more piece of information
- C. Nothing. It is already booked in

**R2.** You are about to do something new. Whose voice turns up first?
- A. Mine, listing what could go wrong
- B. Somebody else's
- C. No voice in particular

**R3.** When you call something realistic, how often is it also true?
- A. Less often than I would like
- B. Usually
- C. Always. I mean it literally

**R4.** Something you had planned falls through and you are quietly relieved. How often?
- A. More than I would admit
- B. Occasionally
- C. Rarely

## AI · AUTHORSHIP
*Not fear and not skill. What you hand it, and what you let it decide.*

**A1.** Before you ask it something, how clear are you on what you actually want?
- A. I work that out by asking
- B. Roughly clear
- C. Clear first, then I ask

**A2.** You ask it something and what comes back is better than what you had. What happens next?
- A. I use it, mostly as it came
- B. I rework it until it sounds like me
- C. I do not ask it things like that

**A3.** When did you last disagree with something it told you?
- A. I cannot remember disagreeing
- B. Recently enough
- C. Regularly. It is part of how I use it

**A4.** Something you made with it gets praised. Which is closer?
- A. A small flicker of something uncomfortable
- B. I take the compliment
- C. I say how it was made

---

## Notes for choosing

**A1 is the one I would keep above the other three.** It is the brand's own
thesis asked as a question — *AI gives back what you hand it, so get clear
first* — and it sorts people without ever mentioning skill.

**A4 does double duty.** It reads as an AI question and scores as measuring,
so it covers two territories in one slot if you are tight on questions.

**S3 and M3 are the two sharpest.** Both make someone stop before answering,
which is the tell that a question is working.

**R1 and R2 overlap** more than the others — both are about what sits in front
of a decision. Take one.

**Keep two per territory.** One answer can be a fluke; two in the same place is
a pattern, and the result reads her own words back to her, so it needs a second
answer to draw from.
