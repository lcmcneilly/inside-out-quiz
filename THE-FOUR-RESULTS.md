# INSIDE OUT QUIZ — THE FOUR RESULTS

One result per pattern. Eight questions: five reflections and three on AI.

Every result has the same five parts, so they read as one family.
Second person throughout — no gendered language anywhere.

---

## What decides which result

Each pattern is scored as a share of its own maximum, because Judgement has two questions and the others have one each. Highest share names the result.

## What decides the door

Two conditions, both required for **Start at SHE IS AI**:

1. **Steady** — no reflection answer at the far end, and low total load.
2. **Authored** — all three AI answers at the strong end: clear before asking, own thinking in the output, and trusting the no.

Anything else is **Start at SHE IS SOL**. SOL is the default on purpose — capability handed to somebody who is not steady only moves faster in the wrong direction.

Because the AI door needs nothing running loud, it only ever appears alongside *“none of the four ran loud”*. The two halves can never contradict each other.

## Notes

**Judgement has two questions**, so it has two possible read-backs. The others have one each.

**Judgement is also the default** when nothing separates the four. That is the only arbitrary bit in the whole thing, and the *nothing separated* line says so plainly rather than pretending.

**The names are blunt.** Jealousy and Neediness are hard words to hand somebody who has known you for four minutes. They are the course's own names and they match the Sol Map, which is the argument for keeping them — but if you want them softened for cold traffic, that is one table to change.
