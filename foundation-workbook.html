What this is

This is the ground you stand on before the course begins — yours to work with for as long as you like, and the foundation everything in the course is built on. It's one practice, done nightly, that clears the noise and brings you back to what was always there.

How the practice works

Each night, before sleep, you visualise a pyramid of a single colour in your mind, and let it clear you. It takes three to five minutes. The brain doesn't distinguish a vividly imagined experience from a real one — the same pathways fire — so the colour does real work.

  1. 01Close your eyes. Settle your body. Take three slow breaths.
  2. 02See a pyramid of your colour in your mind, apex pointing up. Let it glow.
  3. 03Set the intention silently — the words for that colour, or simply "please clear me."
  4. 04Rest in the image for one to three minutes. Let it work.
  5. 05Let go, and sleep.

The rhythm — seven, six, three

Seven nights with one colour. Then the next. Six colours make one full round; three rounds make your first eighteen weeks. Red comes last in every round — presence built on everything already cleared. By the third round the practice becomes intuitive: not a programme you follow, but something you reach for.

How to move through these modules

Work through Module 1 to Module 6 in order — Purple, Blue, Green, Orange, Turquoise, Red. There's no rush and no way to fall behind. Seven nights each. Begin when you're ready. Each written module has a short video to watch alongside it — read it, watch it, then work the practice for the week.

A few practical notes
When.

Do the pyramid at night, as you settle for sleep — that's when it clears most deeply. The daytime drill you do in the moment, whenever the centre is needed.

How long.

Three to five minutes is plenty. This is not about effort or getting it perfect; it's about showing up each night.

Consistency over intensity.

Seven gentle nights with one colour does more than one long, forced session. If you miss a night, simply begin again the next — nothing is broken.

Notice what surfaces.

Keep a note of what comes up as you work each colour — a feeling, a memory, a knowing. You'll bring this awareness into the course.

What comes next

These six modules are your foundation. When the weekly calls and the eighteen-week course begin, everything builds on this nightly practice — so keep it going. You'll arrive already cleared, already listening inward, already home in the practice. That is the whole point of beginning here.

A gentle word

Go gently with yourself. This work is a returning, not a race — and sometimes what's been held quietly for a long time begins to move. If something heavy surfaces, you don't have to move through it alone. Rest, take your time, and lean on the people around you. You also have the whole community with you here — there's a space where you can reach out and ask for support at any time, and someone will be there. That is what it's for.

Work with Red for seven nights — and you've closed one full round of the six. Begin again at Purple. Three rounds is your first eighteen weeks, and by the third the practice is no longer a programme you follow but a tool you reach for.
