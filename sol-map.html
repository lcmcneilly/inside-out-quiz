The Pyramid of Creation

What you are building on

Everything you make stands on four layers, and they stack in one order only. Trust is the ground. Belief is the direction. Emotion is the engine. Thought is the blueprint.

Where results won't come, one of these four is the real block — and it is almost never the one you've been working on.

What you'll actually work through

Six lights, seven nights, three times

The practice underneath everything is one nightly visualisation, three to five minutes as you settle for sleep. Seven nights with one colour, then the next. Six colours make a round; three rounds make the first eighteen weeks — one hundred and twenty-six nights exactly.

The order never changes and it isn't personalised. Red is always last, because presence built on everything already cleared is solid rather than reactive. Nothing you answered above moves your place in it.
