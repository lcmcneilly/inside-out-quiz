Inside Out

What happens to your details

Short version: we take your first name and your email so we can send you your quiz result and write to you before the doors open. That is all we take, and you can have it removed whenever you want.

Who is collecting it

SHE IS SOL and SHE IS AI, trading as Inside Out, in New Zealand. We are the ones responsible for it. Reach us at hello@sheisai.ai.

What we take

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • Which of the four patterns your answers pointed to, and which door
  • That you joined through the quiz, and the date

Your eight answers are not sent anywhere. They are worked out in your own browser and stay there. What leaves the page is the one word the answers produced, not the answers themselves.

Why we take it

To send you the email that carries your result, to write to you between now and the doors opening on 31 October, and to hold your founding membership at the price you joined at. Nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it, or hand it to anybody for their own marketing.

Where it is held

In Ivorey, the platform Inside Out runs on. Ivorey stores data on servers outside New Zealand, which means your name and email are held overseas under that provider's own protections. If you would rather that did not happen, do not use the form and email us instead, and we will tell you what we can do without it.

Giving it is up to you

Nothing on the quiz requires it. You can take all eight questions and read your whole result without entering anything. The form only exists because the waitlist and the result email cannot work without somewhere to send them.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until the waitlist has clearly finished with you — whichever comes first. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and using it stops the emails. If you want the record itself gone rather than just the emails stopped, say so and we will delete it.

Seeing it and correcting it

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask us what we hold about you and have it corrected if it is wrong. Email hello@sheisai.ai and we will come back to you within twenty working days, which is what the Act allows us. There is no charge.

If you are not happy with us

Tell us first and we will try to sort it. If that does not work, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
