# INSIDE OUT — THE TEN QUESTIONS

For review. In the order she meets them.

Seven reflections and three on AI, interleaved on purpose so the AI three don't land as a block at the end.

**Nothing is named in the result** — no pattern, no archetype, no label. The italic note under each question is internal only; she never sees it.

---

## 1. Someone tells you what they have decided to do with their life. How long before you know what you think of it?

*reflection · the verdict, and how fast it arrives*

- **A.** I know before they have finished the sentence
- **B.** I usually need a minute
- **C.** I am often still deciding days later

## 2. Someone doing what you do gets the thing you wanted. What happens in the first second, before you have chosen how to feel?

*reflection · the measuring*

- **A.** Something tightens
- **B.** Not much either way
- **C.** I am glad, and it is not complicated

## 3. When you use AI on something that matters, whose thinking ends up in the finished thing?

*on AI · authorship*

- **A.** Mostly mine. It speeds up work I had already decided on
- **B.** A mix, and I could not always tell you where the line is
- **C.** I do not use it for anything that matters

## 4. How much can one conversation change how you feel about an entire day?

*reflection · where steadiness is kept*

- **A.** Completely. It sets the tone
- **B.** Somewhat
- **C.** Barely

## 5. At the end of an ordinary day, which is closer?

*reflection · the verdict, and how fast it arrives*

- **A.** I run back through how I did
- **B.** It depends on the day
- **C.** The day just ends

## 6. What comes back from it is vague. Where do you look first?

*on AI · authorship*

- **A.** At what I asked for
- **B.** At the tool
- **C.** I have not thought about it that way

## 7. Is there anyone whose updates you have quietly stopped looking at?

*reflection · the measuring*

- **A.** Yes, and I know exactly why
- **B.** Maybe one or two
- **C.** No

## 8. Think of something you have wanted and not started. The reason you give yourself — how honest is it?

*reflection · the reasons given out loud*

- **A.** It is a reason I would defend out loud. I am not sure I believe it
- **B.** Partly true
- **C.** It is the actual reason

## 9. Have you ever taken an answer from it that you had a quiet feeling was wrong?

*on AI · authorship*

- **A.** Yes, and I went with it anyway
- **B.** Yes, and I went and checked
- **C.** Not that I have noticed

## 10. You do something generous and nobody acknowledges it. What happens next?

*reflection · where steadiness is kept*

- **A.** It sits with me longer than I would like
- **B.** A flicker, then it is gone
- **C.** Nothing. I had already forgotten

---

## How the answers are used

**The seven** decide which two of her answers are read back to her word for word, and which of four plain-language *shape* lines is true of them. No pattern is named — the Sol Map does that.

**The three** route her to a door. Discernment is a gate rather than a score: answering *"Yes, and I went with it anyway"* sends her to SOL whatever the other two say.

She never sees a score, a number or a label.
