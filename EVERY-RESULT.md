# INSIDE OUT QUIZ — EVERY RESULT

Ten results. That is the whole space the seven questions can produce.

Each is five parts: **the line**, **the answer read back**, **the question**, **the Sol Map line**, **the door**.
Edit anything in place and hand it back. No gendered language anywhere — every result is written in second person.

---

## How they combine

| Part | Variants | Decided by |
|---|---|---|
| The line | 4 | the territory scoring highest as a share of its own maximum |
| The answer read back | 4, or none | the strongest answer in that territory |
| The question | 4 | same as the line |
| The Sol Map line | 2 | whether anything scored at all |
| The door | 2 | all three AI answers strong, or not |

The verdict territory has two questions, so it has two possible quotes. The others have one each.
The AI door needs all three AI answers at the strong end; anything less points to SOL, because clarity comes first.
