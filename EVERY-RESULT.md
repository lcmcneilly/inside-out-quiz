# INSIDE OUT QUIZ — EVERY RESULT

Ten complete results. That is the whole space the seven questions can produce.

Each one is five slots: **the line**, **her own answer back**, **the question**, **the Sol Map line**, **the door**.
Edit any of them in place and hand this back.

---

## How they combine

| Slot | Variants | Decided by |
|---|---|---|
| The line | 4 | which territory scored highest as a share of its own maximum |
| Her answer back | 4, or none | the strongest answer in that territory |
| The question | 4 | same as the line |
| The Sol Map line | 2 | whether anything scored at all |
| The door | 2 | the three AI questions |

Verdict has two questions, so it has two possible quotes. The others have one each.
