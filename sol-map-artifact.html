Who are you?

Welcome to the Sol Map. It takes about fifteen minutes, and will give you a personalised map to begin with, based on your answers and your birth details.

It is a baseline, not a rank. Nothing in it is scored, graded, or compared to anybody else, and there is no good or bad result. Some of the questions are uncomfortable to answer honestly. Answer them with the first thing that comes to your mind.

Before you start

What SHE IS SOL is

A self-led system for people ready to meet themselves honestly. It has a shape and a rhythm, and you run it yourself. The whole wellness industry runs on you are not enough yet. This runs on the opposite: you are not deficient, you have forgotten. The work was never about becoming, it was always about returning.

Underneath it is one practice. Six colours of light, seven nights each, three times round — a hundred and twenty-six nights, three to five minutes before sleep. That is the engine, and it is deliberately small enough to actually keep.

The room

Lumina

Lumina is where SHE IS SOL and SHE IS AI meet — two doors into one community. SOL is about knowing yourself. SHE IS AI will help you use AI as a tool. The space is here for you to recognise how much power you have, and to build something with it.

They sit in one room on purpose. When you build from the foundation of knowing yourself first, then the tools are worth having.

What your answers show

The four horsemen

Judgement, jealousy, neediness and fear. Left unobserved they take a life apart slowly, and none of them announce themselves — each one arrives dressed as something reasonable. Their counters are what build it back.

Nothing below is a score or a type. It is a reading of what is loudest in you this month, which is a different thing entirely — and it is meant to be argued with.

The other three are in the room too. This is the question each one is asking, whether or not you're answering it.

The Pyramid of Creation

What is holding it up

Everything you make stands on three layers, and they stack in one order only. Trust and belief are the ground. Emotion is the energy. Thought and intent are the blueprint.

Where results won't come, one of these three is the real block — and it is almost never the one you've been working on.
