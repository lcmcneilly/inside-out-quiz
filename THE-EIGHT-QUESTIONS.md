# THE EIGHT QUESTIONS

The Inside Out funnel quiz, in the order they appear on screen. Five reflections
and three on AI, interleaved so the AI ones never arrive as a block.

Nothing is shown to the reader except the question and the three answers. The
territory names and the scoring are internal and never appear.

---

## 1.

**At the end of an ordinary day, which is closer?**

- I run back through how I did
- It depends on the day
- The day just ends

*Territory: the verdict. First answer is the loud one.*

---

## 2.

**Before you ask AI something, how clear are you on what you actually want?**

- I work that out by asking
- Roughly clear
- Clear first, then I ask

*AI. Third answer is the strong one. This is the brand's own thesis asked as a
question — AI gives back what you hand it, so get clear first.*

---

## 3.

**You open your phone and the first thing you see is somebody else's good news. What do you do?**

- Keep moving past it
- Look, then carry on
- Stop and read it properly

*Territory: the measuring. First answer is the loud one. Scrolling past is the
tell, not the polite response.*

---

## 4.

**When you use AI on something that matters, whose thinking ends up in the finished thing?**

- Mostly mine. It speeds up work I had already decided on
- A mix, and I could not always tell you where the line is
- I do not use it for anything that matters

*AI. First answer is the strong one. The third is not a safe answer — opting out
is not authorship either.*

---

## 5.

**How much can one conversation change how you feel about an entire day?**

- Completely. It sets the tone
- Somewhat
- Barely

*Territory: where steadiness is kept. First answer is the loud one.*

---

## 6.

**Somebody asks why you have not done the thing yet. What comes out of your mouth?**

- The reason I always give
- Something roughly honest
- A date

*Territory: fear. First answer is the loud one. A date is the settled answer,
because it is the only one that is not a reason.*

---

## 7.

**You hear how someone else handled something difficult. What turns up first?**

- What I would have done instead
- A question about why
- Nothing much. It was theirs to handle

*Territory: the verdict, second question. First answer is the loud one.*

---

## 8.

**When AI gives you an answer and something in you says no, what usually happens?**

- I go with it anyway. It is easier than arguing with it
- Depends how much time I have
- I trust the no, and argue with it

*AI. Third answer is the strong one. Discernment, which is the one the other two
AI questions cannot test.*

---

## How the answers turn into a result

**The pattern.** Four territories, four results: Judgement, Jealousy, Neediness,
Fear. The verdict carries two questions and the others carry one each, so each
territory is scored as a share of its own maximum rather than a raw total.
Otherwise the verdict would win almost every time.

**The quoted line.** Whichever answer sat at the far end gets read back in the
reader's own words. If no answer sat at the far end there is nothing to quote,
and one of two replacement lines takes its place.

**The door.** SHE IS SOL is the default. SHE IS AI needs both conditions at once:
no answer at the far end on any of the five reflections, and all three AI answers
at the strong end. Capability handed to somebody who is not steady only moves
faster in the wrong direction.
